# Home Ustav (1910), the zapevy for the whole year (fol. 209-225v): English translation.
# Built into data/ustav.json by tools/build_ustav.py, with home-ustav.txt.
# A zapev is the refrain said with each prostration, or with each prayer on the lestovka.
#
#   = key | text                  a zapev for the days of the week, used as [zapev key]
#   WHEN KIND | text              a zapev for a date of the year
#     WHEN:  M-D                  this church (Julian) date
#            M-D..M-D             these dates (a forefeast, a feast and its afterfeast)
#            sunday M-D..M-D      the Sunday that falls between these dates
#            pascha N  /  N..M    this many days from Pascha (negative: before)
#     KIND:  feast                the feast of the day; no saint is taken from the calendar
#            season               a forefeast, afterfeast or lesser feast; the saint of the day is still prayed to
#            saint                the saint of the day; takes the place of the calendar's saint
#
# [zapev saint] in a prayer gives the date's zapevy; where the book has none, it is
# made from the calendar's saint after the forms of the General Menaion (fol. 209v-210v):
#   O holy prophet N. / apostle N. / martyr N. / great-martyr N. / hieromartyr N. /
#   monastic martyr N. … ; O hierarch of Christ N. ; O venerable father N. ;
#   O holy and righteous N. ; O holy right-believing prince N. ; O holy passion-bearer N. …,
#   pray to God for us.
# Where the book gives a second zapev ("Or:", "Another:"), the first is used; the
# others are kept here as comments.
