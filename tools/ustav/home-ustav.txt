=== entrance-opening | The Entrance Opening
God, be merciful to me, a sinner. [bow entrance]
Thou who hast created me, O Lord, have mercy on me. [bow entrance]
Without number have I sinned; O Lord, have mercy, and forgive me a sinner. [bow entrance]
It is truly meet to bless thee, O God-bearer, ever blessed and most unblemished, and the Mother of our God. More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow great]
! This great prostration is never left out.
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost. [bow entrance]
Both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen. [bow entrance]
Lord, have mercy. Lord, have mercy. Lord, bless. [bow entrance]
! Then the dismissal:
O Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, for the sake of the prayers of thy most pure Mother, and of my holy guardian angel, and of all thy saints: O Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me and save me, for thou art good and the lover of mankind.

But deliver me from the evil one, and let thy will be done in me. [bow]

=== prayer-having-risen | Prayer: Having risen from sleep
! Prayer.
Having risen from sleep, I thank thee, O all-holy Trinity, that for the sake of thy great goodness and longsuffering thou wast not angered with me, thy sinful and slothful servant, neither hast thou destroyed me with mine iniquities, but didst show love for mankind; and when I lay in despair, thou didst raise me up to keep the morning office and to glorify thine invincible dominion. And now, O Master, O most holy God, enlighten the eyes of my heart, and open my lips to be instructed in thy words, and to understand thy commandments, and to do thy will, and to sing unto thee in heartfelt confession, even to sing forth and glorify thy most honorable and majestic name, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== creed | The Creed
I believe in one God the Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, and of all things visible and invisible. And in one Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, the Only-begotten, who was begotten of the Father before all ages; Light from Light, true God from true God, begotten, but not created; of one essence with the Father, by whom all things came to be. Who for us men and for our salvation came down from the heavens, and was made flesh of the Holy Ghost and of the Virgin Mary, became man. Who was crucified for us under Pontius Pilate, suffered and was buried. And rose on the third day according to the Scriptures. And ascended into the heavens, and sitteth at the right hand of the Father. And is coming again with glory to judge the living and the dead; whose kingdom has no end.
! Of the Second Council.
And in the Holy Ghost, the Lord, the true and life-giving, who proceedeth from the Father; who with the Father and the Son together is worshipped and glorified; who spake by the prophets. And in one holy, conciliar and apostolic Church. I confess one baptism unto the remission of sins. I look for the resurrection of the dead, and the life of the age to come. Amen.

=== prayer-of-forgiveness | Prayer of forgiveness: Remit, pardon
! Make a [bow word] and say this prayer of forgiveness:
Remit, pardon and forgive, O God, our transgressions, both voluntary and involuntary, in word and in deed, in knowledge and in ignorance, in mind and in thought, by day and by night: forgive me them all, for thou art good and the lover of mankind.

=== forgive-them-that-hate | Prayer: Forgive them that hate and wrong us
! Rise and say this prayer:
Forgive them that hate and wrong us, O Lord, thou lover of mankind. Do good unto them that do good. Grant unto our brethren and all our kinsmen, even unto them that have withdrawn into solitude, all their petitions that are unto salvation, and life eternal. {(bow)}
Visit and heal them that are in sickness. Set free them that are in prison. Be thou the pilot of them that sail upon the waters. Direct and prosper them that journey by the way. {(bow)}
Remember, O Lord, our captive brethren of one faith with us, of the Orthodox faith, and deliver them from every evil circumstance. {(bow)}
Have mercy, O Lord, on them that have given us alms, and have charged us, the unworthy, to pray for them: forgive them and have mercy on them. {(bow)}
Have mercy, O Lord, on them that labor and serve us, that show us mercy and feed us, and grant them all their petitions that are unto salvation, and life eternal. {(bow)}
Remember, O Lord, our fathers and brethren that have departed before us, and make them to dwell where the light of thy countenance shineth. {(bow)}
Remember also, O Lord, our lowliness and poverty, and enlighten our mind with the light of the understanding of thy holy Gospel, and guide us in the path of thy commandments; through the prayers of thy most pure Mother and of all thy saints. Amen. {(bow)}

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- cell rule

=== cell-twelve-prostrations | Cell rule: the twelve opening prostrations
! Stand in your usual place. With humility of heart and a contrite mind, make these bows, saying:
O God, cleanse me a sinner, and have mercy on me. [bow rule]
Thou who hast created me, O Lord, have mercy on me. [bow rule]
Without number have I sinned; O Lord, forgive me. [bow rule]
O God, be merciful to me a sinner. [bow rule]
O God, forgive my iniquities and transgressions. [bow rule]
O my most holy Lady, God-bearer, have mercy on me and save me, and help me now in this life, and at the departure of my soul, and in the age to come. [bow rule]
O invincible and divine power of the honorable and life-giving Cross of the Lord, forsake me not, a sinner who hopeth in thee. [bow rule]
All ye heavenly powers, holy angels and archangels, cherubim and seraphim, have mercy on me, and pray for me unto the Lord God, and help me now in this life, and at the departure of my soul, and in the age to come. [bow rule]
O angel of Christ, my holy guardian, have mercy on me, and pray for me a sinner unto the Lord God, and help me now in this life, and at the departure of my soul, and in the age to come. [bow rule]
O holy and great John, prophet and forerunner of the Lord, have mercy on me, and pray for me unto the Lord God, and help me now in this life, and at the departure of my soul, and in the age to come. [bow rule]
O holy and glorious apostles, prophets and martyrs, hierarchs, venerable and righteous ones, and all ye saints, have mercy on me, and pray for me a sinner unto the Lord God, and help me now in this life, and at the departure of my soul, and in the age to come. [bow rule]
O Lord, whether in word, or in deed, or in thought I have sinned in all my life, have mercy on me and forgive me, for thy mercy's sake. [bow rule]

=== prayer-macarius | Prayer of St Macarius
! The prayer of Saint Macarius.
O God, cleanse me a sinner, for I have never done any good thing before thee; but deliver me from the evil one, and let thy will be done in me; that without condemnation I may open my unworthy mouth, and praise thy holy name, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== prayer-isaac-the-syrian | Prayer of St Isaac the Syrian
! The second prayer, of Saint Isaac the Syrian.
O Lord Jesus Christ my God, who visitest thy creation: my passions are manifest unto thee, and the weakness of my nature, and the strength of mine adversary. Do thou thyself, O Master, shield me from his malice; for his strength is mighty, and my nature is subject to passion, and my strength is feeble. Thou therefore who art good, who knowest my weakness and bearest the burden of my feebleness, keep me from troubled thoughts and from the flood of the passions, and make me worthy of this holy service; lest by my passions I corrupt the sweetness thereof, and be found shameless and presumptuous before thee. But in thy mercy have mercy upon me; for blessed art thou unto the ages. Amen.

=== prayer-stephen-of-thebes | Prayer of St Stephen of Thebes
! The third prayer, of Saint Stephen of Thebes.
O Master, Lord Jesus Christ my God, be thou my helper, for I am in thy hands. Suffer me not to sin, for I am led astray. Suffer me not to follow mine evil will. Suffer me not to perish in my sins. Have compassion on thy creature. Cast me not away from thy face because of my sins, for unto thee have I fled. Heal my soul, for I have sinned against thee. Before thee are all they that afflict me, and that seek after my soul to take it; and I have no other refuge but thee alone, O Lord. O Lord, save me for thy mercy's sake, for thou, O Lord, art mighty in all things. For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, with the Father and the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== prayer-chrysostom | Prayer of St John Chrysostom
! The fourth prayer, of Saint John Chrysostom.
O Lord, whether I will or no, save me. For I, as mire that loveth filth, desire the defilement of sin; but thou, being good and almighty, canst restrain me. For if thou have mercy upon the righteous, it is no great thing; and if thou save the pure, it is no marvel, for they are worthy of thy mercy. But upon me, O Master, the wretched and sinful and defiled, show forth the wonder of thy mercy, and make manifest thy loving-kindness; for unto thee am I left, a beggar, made poor of all good works. O Lord, save me for thy mercy's sake, for blessed art thou unto the ages. Amen.

=== prayer-fifth-cell | The fifth prayer of the cell rule
! The fifth prayer.
O Master, have mercy on me for thy goodness' sake, and suffer me not to stray from thy will. And turn not away my poor prayer from thy face, but hear, O Lord, the voice of my prayer when I pray unto thee by day and by night, and receive it as choice incense. And because of my sins withhold not thy grace, but save me for the sake of thy holy name. For thine alone it is to have mercy on us and to save us, and unto thee we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== cross-blessing | Blessing with the cross
! Having prayed and first cleansed yourself, kiss the cross that you wear on your breast, sign yourself with it, and say:
O Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, bless and sanctify and preserve me by the power of thy life-giving Cross.

=== god-bearer-prayers | Prayers to the God-bearer
! Then make 100 waist bows with the prayer:
My most holy Lady, God-bearer, save me a sinner.
! The book sets these 100 after the full rule of 300 prostrations to the ground and 600 Jesus Prayers. For anyone who cannot keep that, it gives shorter forms, each with fewer prayers to the God-bearer:
! 200 prostrations and 400 Jesus Prayers, then 100 to the God-bearer;
! or 100 prostrations and 200 Jesus Prayers, then 50 to the God-bearer;
! or, at the least, 50 prostrations and 100 Jesus Prayers, with no number given for the God-bearer.

=== it-is-truly-meet | It is truly meet
It is truly meet to bless thee, O God-bearer, ever blessed and most unblemished, and the Mother of our God. More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify.

=== cell-troparia | Troparia: Have mercy on us, O Lord
! Troparia, Tone 6.
Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us; for, being at a loss for any defense, this prayer do we sinners offer unto thee as Master: have mercy on us.
{Glory:} O Lord, have mercy on us, for in thee have we put our trust. Be not exceeding wroth with us, neither remember our iniquities; but look upon us as one compassionate, and deliver us from our enemies. For thou art our God, and we are thy people; we are all the work of thy hands, and we call upon thy name.
{Both now:} Open unto us the doors of compassion, O blessed God-bearer and Virgin, that hoping in thee we perish not, but through thee may be delivered from adversities; for thou art the salvation of the Christian race.

=== ending-cell-rule | Ending of the cell rule
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times)}
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify.
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== additions-hours-sunday | Daily additions: the Hours on Sunday
! On Sunday, the day of the Resurrection, after the kathismas or lestovkas, pray first to the Resurrection, and then to the God-bearer and to the saint who falls on that date.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-1 | After the 1st kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
Of blessedness and of godliness have I been deprived, and into the way of ungodliness have I turned aside; in the nature of my mind have I gone astray, and of the fruit of faith, of well-planted understanding, have I been bereft. O Planter of good things, and Instructor of the foolish, by the power of thy Cross save me.
{Glory:} From the mind that troubleth me, and from wrathful thought, deliver me, O Lord. Fearing the fury of thy threatening, with words of anguish I cry unto thee: snatch me from all the evils that have settled in me, and save me.
{Both now:} O most pure God-bearer, blessed in heaven and glorified on earth: rejoice, O ever-virgin Bride.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The first prayer.
O God almighty, righteous and all-praised; O God great and mighty; O God before the ages: hear me, thy sinful servant, in this hour. Hear me, O God, and hearken ever unto my lowliness. Hear me, O Lord, as I pray unto thee, as once thou didst hear thy prophet Elias; and by his prayers burn up mine iniquities, and cleanse my sins, O God, Creator of the holy bodiless powers, who hast said: Ask, and ye shall receive. Disdain not to hear me, who am held fast by sins and offer prayer with unclean lips. Yea, O Lord, who hast promised to hear them that call upon thee in truth, direct my steps into the way of peace, and forgive me all my transgressions, voluntary and involuntary, O thou who hast said: Be ye harmless as doves. I have cried with my whole heart; hear me, O Lord, thou hope of all the ends of the earth, and of them that are afar off upon the sea. Rebuke the unclean spirits from before the face of mine infirmity. Take up weapon and shield, and stand up to help me; draw out the sword, and stop the way against them that persecute me. Say unto my soul: I am thy salvation. Let the spirit of pride depart from mine infirmity, and the spirit of hatred and of envy, the spirit of fear and of despair, of folly and of all malice. Let every burning and stirring be quenched in me that ariseth from the workings of the devil. Let my soul and body be enlightened by the Spirit unto understanding, by thy light and by the multitude of thy compassions. Let thy mercy dwell upon me, through the prayers of our most holy Lady the God-bearer, and of all thy saints; that I may glorify thy most holy name, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-2 | After the 2nd kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
Make haste to open unto me thy fatherly embrace, for riotously have I spent my life. But looking upon the unfailing riches of thy compassions, O Saviour, despise not now my heart made poor, O Lord; for unto thee I cry in repentance: Father, I have sinned against heaven and before thee.
{Glory:} I have emulated the deeds of the prodigal, but have not gained his repentance. But as thou art most good, O Lord, bring me back, who have willingly gone astray, and cleanse me from the defilement of my transgressions, for the God-bearer's sake, as I cry unto thee: Father, I have sinned against heaven and before thee.
{Both now:} Receive the prayer of thy servants, O Virgin God-bearer, and deliver us from every calamity; for thou hast borne Christ the Saviour, the Deliverer of our souls.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The second prayer.
O Lord Jesus Christ, our God and Saviour, Treasury of good things: grant me the perfection of repentance, and a heart that grieveth, that with all my soul I may go forth to seek thee, who art all good. For besides thee I shall fear none. And grant me, O Good One, thy grace that loveth mankind. May the Father, who before the ages brought thee forth from his bosom, timeless and co-eternal, renew in me the likeness of thine image. I have forsaken thee; forsake not thou me. I have gone out from thee; go out thou to seek me, and lead me into thy pasture, and number me with the sheep of thy chosen flock, and nourish me with them from the pasture of thy divine mysteries. Their dwelling is a pure heart, and in it the pure shining of thy revelations, which thou hast prepared for them that have laboured for thy sake in all afflictions. May we be vouchsafed to obtain them by thy grace and love for mankind; and unto thee we send up glory, with thy Father who is without beginning, and with thy most holy, good and life-giving Spirit, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-3 | After the 3rd kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
Visit my lowly soul, O Lord, which hath spent all its life in sins; but as thou didst the harlot, receive me, and save me.
{Glory:} I am enfeebled by the sleep of despondency, and darkened by the sloth of sin. But shine upon me the light of repentance, O Christ God, enlightening the sight of my mind, O Enlightener of our souls.
{Both now:} We have known the Word of the Father, Christ our God, incarnate of thee, O Virgin God-bearer: him do thou entreat to save our souls.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The third prayer.
O Master, God, Father of all compassions, and Lord of consolation: have compassion on me, thine unworthy servant. Let not the multitude of mine evils overcome thy love for mankind. O my God, thrust me not away from thy goodness, but assuage thine anger toward them that have sinned against thee, for thou art good. Be merciful unto me a sinner, who every day and hour provoke thy good and forbearing name. As thou didst save the harlot, and the publican, and the thief, and him who at the eleventh hour had done nothing worthy, so save me also, the unworthy, who have wrought all evil and every sin, and have never done any good before thee, but have ever followed my wicked and evil deeds. Yet nevertheless, O Master God, repenting I flee unto thy compassions. Let thy mercy go before me, O Lord, and let the almighty power of thy Holy Ghost strengthen me against the unseen demons that ever war with me, that upon me also thy sovereign right hand may be magnified, and thine unsearchable goodness. For the sake of faith, and not of works, save me, thine unworthy servant, and I will praise thee all the days of my life; for thee do all the powers of heaven hymn, the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-4 | After the 4th kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
I have loved earthly and temporal things, and am deprived of eternal good things. But do thou, who alone lovest mankind, direct the thought of my mind unto thyself, and save my poor soul from sins.
{Glory:} Receive me as I repent before thee, as thou didst the thief and the harlot; and despise me not as I turn again, but forgive me all my sins, known and unknown, O thou who alone art plenteous in mercy.
{Both now:} I weep every hour for the fall of my soul, and call upon thee, O spotless Virgin, to help me. O merciful Lady, Virgin God-bearer, deliver my lowly soul from eternal torment.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The fourth prayer.
O God and Father, who alone art good and the lover of mankind, who alone art true and righteous, who alone art compassionate and merciful, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who was crucified under Pontius Pilate, suffered and was buried, and rose on the third day, and ascended into the heavens in his glory: let the power of thy majesty come upon me, thy sinful servant, and strengthen me by the good tidings of thy teaching. Do thou, O Master, enlighten mine inward parts and all my members by thy will, and cleanse me from all malice and sin. Keep me undefiled and blameless from every working of the devil, and grant me, according to thy goodness, to think the things that are thine, to understand the things that are thine, to live in thy will, to walk in thy fear, and to do the things well-pleasing unto thee, until my last day and hour and breath. And I call upon thee, O Lord Jesus Christ, I the sinful, unworthy and unclean, that according to thine unspeakable mercy thou wouldest keep my soul, and body, and mind from every snare of the devil. Yea, O holy Father, cover me with thy mercy, and forsake me not, lest mine enemies say: We have swallowed him up. And my song shall be of thee unto the ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-5 | After the 5th kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
Grant me, O Lord, the watchfulness of the wise virgins, a bright lamp for my soul; and enlighten me with the light of thy compassions, that I may cry unto thee the angelic song: Alleluia.
{Glory:} Have mercy upon me, said David; and I cry unto thee: I have sinned, O Saviour; cleanse my sins by repentance, and have mercy upon me.
{Both now:} O spotless Bride, Mother of the Word, from thy seedless womb thou hast caused the light to shine upon us who were darkened; wherefore all we faithful magnify thee.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The fifth prayer.
O Lord our God, Father of the true Light, who hast adorned the day with heavenly light, and enlightenest the night with fiery rays; who hast prepared the rest of the age to come for them that love thee, unto life eternal: enlighten our hearts with unceasing light unto true understanding, and keep our life without stumbling, granting us to walk honestly, as in the day, and to glorify thy most honorable and majestic name, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-6 | After the 6th kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
I have not the sighing of the publican, nor the tears of repentance of the harlot; in despair I perish grievously. But I cry unto thee, O God: have compassion and have mercy upon me.
{Glory:} For thou art a merciful God, and the Father of consolation: hearken unto me as I pray unto thee, O Giver of light to them that are in darkness. O Lord, glory to thee.
{Both now:} Thou art, O God-bearer, a pillar of strength to the faithful; thou art a deep of mercy that cannot be drained. Wherefore save them that hymn thee, O all-glorious one.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The sixth prayer.
O God-bearer, Queen and Sovereign, Lady Virgin Mary, Mother of our God: thy Son, the God of all, despised not the sinners that fled unto him, but saved them all, and brought them to the knowledge of the truth. But I, the wretched, have defiled my baptism, and transgressed the vows that were laid upon me, and have not kept the commandments of thy Son and my God. But unto thee, O Lady, have I fled. Teach me to do the will of thy Son, my Lord, that he may save me, and bring me to the knowledge of the truth, and grant me the grace of the Holy Ghost, that I be not caught in the nets of the enemy. Other help have I found none from my youth, save thee, O Lady God-bearer. Oft have I angered thee, yet thou didst not abhor me. But I, the wretched, am insensible of thy mercy, and have fallen into the depth of folly, and have destroyed my understanding. Yet unto thee I cry without ceasing: have mercy on me, O Sovereign Lady, and raise me up from the abyss of sins. Stretch out thy hand to me that lie fallen; strengthen me, the infirm; cleanse me, the defiled; save me, who am perishing; that all sinners, seeing thy mercy upon me, the unworthy, may flee unto thy protection, and glorify thy Son, with the Father and with the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-7 | After the 7th kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
O my soul, why art thou slothful, and servest sin? Why, being sick, dost thou not run unto the Physician? Awake now from the evils that thou hast done, and cry unto the Saviour, saying: O Hope of the hopeless, Life of them that have despaired, O Physician, my Saviour, have mercy upon me.
{Glory:} Thou shalt be brought, O my soul, to the judgment of all the world, and dragged away suddenly by the fiery angels. Whom then wilt thou call to thy help, having thine evil deeds to convict thee? Awake now, and repent, crying unto Christ God: O lover of mankind, save me.
{Both now:} Heal me, O God-bearer, who am sick from evil deeds, and lighten my pains, granting me the medicine of repentance, O Mother of Christ God.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The seventh prayer.
I thank thee, O Lord our God, for all thy benefits, which have been upon us, the unworthy, from the first day until this present hour. And now, O Lord, unto thee I stretch forth my mind, and heart, and thought, and send up all my senses. Cleanse me, O Merciful One, cleanse me; for wholly condemned I come unto thee, and without an answer I flee unto thy mercy. Unto thee, as one ashamed and without an advocate, I bow the neck of my heart and body. I lift up defiled hands, and weep with sighing, and pray with compunction; from the depth of my soul I cry: Cleanse me, O Lord, cleanse me, and deliver me from the unquenchable fire. And give me not, the wretched, to be a rejoicing to the demon that shamelessly assaileth me. Therefore, O Lord, make haste to come quickly and help me, and vouchsafe me to stand without condemnation before thy dread judgment seat, and to obtain the eternal gifts with all thine elect, through the prayers of the God-bearer and of all thy saints. For thou art merciful and longsuffering, and unto thee we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-8 | After the 8th kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
Terrible art thou, O Lord, and who shall stand against thy righteous wrath? Or who shall entreat thee, O Good One? Or who shall appease thee for a people that have sinned and are past hope? Only the heavenly ranks, angels and archangels, dominions, cherubim and seraphim, crying unto thee Hosanna: Holy, holy, holy art thou, O God; have mercy on us.
{Glory:} O Lord, O Lord, all things fear thee, and tremble before the face of thy glory. Before thee we fall down, O Immortal One, and unto thee we pray, O Holy One: save our souls, through the prayers of thy saints.
{Both now:} Thou who art the turning of the sorrowful and the change of the sick, O God-bearer most hymned, peace to them that have no peace, calm to the storm-tossed: save the city and the people, thou sole intercessor of the faithful.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The eighth prayer.
O holy God, Master, heavenly King, Jesus Christ: look down now from thy measureless height, and hear the weeping and the prayer of thy servant; and grant me a river of tears, that I may wash away my sins, which are multiplied exceedingly. For they are numbered with the stars of heaven and the drops of rain, yea, and above the sand of the sea: woe is me, my sins are multiplied. Yet nevertheless I know that thou art merciful, and awaitest the repentance of the sinner, and repentest thee of the evil. Thou didst receive the repentance of the thief, and didst save him for a single word, when he said: Remember me, O Saviour, when thou comest in thy kingdom. Remember me likewise, O Lord, who have not so great a faith; for I have lived his life and done his deeds, and in my mind I stand condemned. O Lord, who didst not despise the tears of the harlot, who made bold, and ran, and took ointment, and poured it upon thine honorable head, upon which the angels dare not look; and embracing thy feet, she kissed them, and wiped them with her hair, and thou didst send her away in peace: so also receive my sinful soul in peace, and give me rest in the bosom of Abraham, and number me in thy chosen flock, that I may see the light of thy countenance, and glorify thy holy name, with the Father and the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-9 | After the 9th kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
My deeds and thoughts, my sins and desires, thou wilt require by thine examining: grant me help unto repentance, and unto a good answer, O Christ God, as the lover of mankind.
{Glory:} O Lord, who didst cleanse Peter's denial by tears, and didst forgive the publican his sins for his sighing: have mercy on me also, O lover of mankind.
{Both now:} Rejoice, thou from whom the unchanging Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us. Rejoice, most pure one, the joy of the apostles and martyrs, and the salvation of the faithful, O Mother of Christ God.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The ninth prayer.
Thou, O Master, lover of mankind, hast made me, and hast honoured me with free will. But I was lifted up, and forgot thy lordship, and transgressed all thy commandments, and fell into the bitter bondage of the devil; and he taught me every sin and evil custom. Yet thou, O Lord my God, hast borne with me until now, awaiting my repentance, and hast not destroyed me while I lived in iniquity. And knowing thy compassion, I have made bold to bring thee prayer with defiled lips. Many sinners hast thou saved; save me also. For they are thy handiwork, and I am thy creature. Though my sins have overtaken me, yet I, the wretched, hope in thy mercy. I pray thee, O Lord, take away from me my will, for all my will is evildoing. For I know that of myself I can do nothing, unless thou give me the understanding of thy grace. The cattle, and the beasts, and the birds of the air have no free will, but as thou madest them, so they abide. But me thou didst make blameless, and I, taking mine own will, have defiled myself wholly. Thou hast given man a will, O Lord; but give it not unto me, for I am a man by nature, but in my ways worse than the beasts. Woe is me, I have shown myself worse than the beasts before thee, O Benefactor. Measure not unto me according to mine evil deeds, but give me time to rise again. Open unto me the doors of repentance; bring me back, the captive, from mine iniquities. Raise me up, who am cast down by the condemnation of sin; draw me out of the pit of defilement. O Jesus Christ, Light of them that are darkened, kindle again in me the light that is quenched. O Physician of the sick, heal my hidden wounds; pouring in oil and wine, bind up my sores. Blot out the handwriting of my sins with the sponge of thy compassion. Grant me, O Lord, to walk in thy fear, and to submit to thy will, that I may be cleansed from sins, and not be possessed by the enemy. And I will sing of thee rejoicing, the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-10 | After the 10th kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
Despise not, O righteous Judge, thy creature, the work of thine own hands, and forsake me not. Though I have sinned as man, more than every man, O Merciful One, yet thou as God hast all power to forgive sins.
{Glory:} The end draweth nigh, O soul, and is close upon thee; yet thou carest not, neither makest thyself ready. The time is shortened: rise up, the Judge is near, even at the doors. As a shadow, and a flower, and a dream, life runneth on. Why, O soul, are we senselessly troubled?
{Both now:} Seeing in myself at every hour the fall of my soul, and despising thy longsuffering, O Word, I have been deprived of all good things, and am become guilty of every torment. But raise me up, the despairing, for the God-bearer's sake, O thou who alone art plenteous in mercy.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The tenth prayer.
O Lord my God, forgive me all my sins, which I have sinned from my youth in all my life. For thou knowest that my will and my desire are toward thee. Oft have I promised thee to repent, and have turned aside into contrary ways. Yet from thee I have not departed, O lover of mankind, neither have I stretched out my hands to a strange god. For thee I know, the one true God, who formed me of the earth, and gavest me the breath of life. Leave me not, O Lord, to be separated from thy saints, and suffer me not to be subject to the passions for ever, nor to be ever like unto the dumb beasts. But raise up my mind from all voluptuousness, and nail my flesh to the fear of thee; and plant it in my heart, which is shaken by evil thoughts, and establish it. Set a door, O Lord, before all my senses. Let mine eyes see virtue alone, and uprightness; let my hearing be instructed by the sweet word of thy commandments; let my taste taste thy testaments, sweeter than honey; my hands, to do thy will; and let my smelling receive the savour of thine ointment, poured forth for our sake, which hath filled all things. Direct my feet into the way of thy will, and soften my soul with compunction, that, walking in the way of thy testimonies and doing what is pleasing unto thee, I may be vouchsafed to obtain the good things promised me, through the prayers of our most holy Lady the God-bearer, and of all thy saints, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-11 | After the 11th kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
As the harlot I fall down before thee, and in place of ointment I bring thee tears from my heart, as she also did. Cleanse me, O Saviour, and grant me forgiveness of sins; for as she, so I also cry unto thee: deliver me from the mire of my deeds, and save me.
{Glory:} My days draw to their end, and the dread throne is made ready; my life floweth past. The judgment awaiteth, threatening me with fire and torment and burning flame. But give me, O Lord, a cloud of tears, that its power may be quenched; for thou willest all men to be saved.
{Both now:} Thee we have as a city and a haven, and an acceptable intercessor with God who was born of thee, O unwedded God-bearer, the salvation of the faithful.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The eleventh prayer.
O God, cleanse me a sinner; take away the rottenness of my soul; scatter the burden laid upon me by the wiles of the evil one. Fence me about by the power of the honorable Cross; establish me by the Holy Ghost. Turn back in shame him that warreth against me, and make me a partaker of thy kingdom, O Christ, true God, through the prayers of our most holy Lady the God-bearer and ever-virgin Mary, and of all thy saints. For blessed art thou, with the Father and the most holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-12 | After the 12th kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
I have not emulated the repentance of the publican, nor have I the tears of the harlot; for through hardness of heart I am unworthy of such amendment. But by thy compassion save me.
{Glory:} O Judge, who knowest secret things and judgest without partiality, deliver me from the torment of flame, for thou alone art compassionate, O Christ God.
{Both now:} Thou hast surpassed the heavenly powers, and wast shown to be the church of God, O blessed God-bearer, who didst bear Christ the Saviour of our souls.
Lord, have mercy. {(forty times; in Great Lent, fifty)}
Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, both now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
More honorable than the cherubim, and truly more glorious than the seraphim, without corruption, having borne God the Word; who art the God-bearer, thee do we magnify. [bow]
! The twelfth prayer.
O Master, Lord almighty, Word of the Father, Jesus Christ, the fulfilment of all things, swift in thy compassions: never departing from thy servants, but ever resting in them, O good Shepherd of rational sheep, deliver me not over to the sedition of the serpent, neither leave me to the desire of Satan, for the seed of corruption is in me. Do thou, O Lord, most holy King, God worshipped, keep me who am slothful, unto the life of light, by thy Holy Ghost, wherewith thou didst sanctify thy disciples, delivering them from the evils of the serpent. Grant, O Lord, the joy of thy salvation upon my bed; and enlighten my mind with the light of thy Gospel, my soul with the love of thee, my Christ, my heart with the purity of thy word, my body with thy passionless Passion. Keep my thoughts in thy peace, and raise me up at a fitting time to glorify thee. For thou art worshipped and glorified, with the Father and the most holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== troparia-after-kathisma-13 | After the 13th kathisma: troparia and prayer
! The penitential troparia.
With the voice of the prodigal cry unto the Lord, O my wretched soul: I have sinned against heaven, O Merciful One, and before thee, as no other. But look upon me with thy gentle eye as I cry: make me as one of thy hired servants, O God, and have mercy.

=== bedtime-first | Before sleep: the first prayer
! The first prayer.
O eternal God and King of all creation, who hast vouchsafed me to attain even unto this hour: forgive me the sins which I have committed this day in word, and deed, and thought; and cleanse, O Lord, my humble soul from all defilement of flesh and spirit. And grant me, O Lord, to pass through the sleep of this night in peace; that rising from my lowly bed, I may be well-pleasing unto thy most holy name all the days of my life, and may trample down the enemies that war against me, both of the flesh and bodiless. Deliver me, O Lord, from vain thoughts that defile me, and from evil lusts. For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== bedtime-second | Before sleep: the second prayer
! The second prayer.
O almighty Word of the Father, who art thyself perfect, Jesus Christ: for the sake of thy great mercy, depart never from thy servants, but abide in them always. O Jesus, good Shepherd of all thy rational sheep, deliver me not over to the sedition of the serpent, neither leave me to the will of Satan, for the seed of corruption is in me. Do thou therefore, O Lord God, worshipped and most holy Jesus Christ, keep me as I sleep by thine unwaning light, and sanctify me by thy Holy Ghost, wherewith thou didst sanctify thy disciples. And grant unto me, thine unworthy servant, the joy of thy salvation upon my bed. Enlighten my mind with the light of the understanding of thy holy Gospel; my soul with the love of thy Cross; my heart with the purity of thy words; my body with thy passionless Passion. Keep my thoughts in thy humility, and raise me up at a fitting time to glorify thee. For thou art most glorified, with the Father and with the Holy Ghost, unto the ages. Amen.

=== bedtime-third | Before sleep: the third prayer
! The third prayer, to the same.
O Lord our God, wherein I have sinned this day, whether in word, or in deed, or in thought, forgive me, for thou art good and the lover of mankind. Grant me peaceful and undisturbed sleep, O God. Send thine angel to be my guardian, to cover and keep me from all evil. For thou art the guardian of our souls and bodies, and unto thee we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== bedtime-angel | Before sleep: prayer to one's own angel
! A prayer to one's own angel.
O angel of Christ, my holy guardian and protector of my soul and body, forgive me all wherein I have sinned against thee this day, and deliver me from every wile of the enemy that opposeth me, that in no sin I may anger my God. But pray for me, a sinful and unworthy servant, that thou mayest show me worthy of the goodness and mercy of the all-holy Trinity, and of the Mother of my God Jesus Christ, and of all the saints, always, now and ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

=== bedtime-cross | Before sleep: prayer to the Cross
! A prayer to the Cross.
Let God arise, and let his enemies be scattered, and let them that hate him flee from before his face. As smoke vanisheth, so let them vanish; as wax melteth before the fire, so let the demons perish from before the face of them that love God and sign themselves with the sign of the Cross; and let us rejoice, saying: Rejoice, Cross of the Lord, who drivest away the demons by the power of our Lord Jesus Christ who was crucified upon thee, who went down into hell and trampled down the power of the devil, and gave us his honorable Cross for the driving away of every adversary. === pascha-eve-rule-note | Great Saturday evening: the rule
! Then pray the rule with prostrations to the ground. After this, eat a little bread. Some other typika direct that bread be eaten from ten o'clock in the evening.

=== pascha-eve-reading | Great Saturday evening: reading until the paschal canon
! After this, until half past ten, read the book of the Acts of the holy apostles. If you have no book of the Acts, read other books, but do not sleep. Whoever cannot read should say the Jesus Prayer in silence.
! If you wish to keep the Great Saturday fast until the end of the tenth hour of the evening, end compline with Glory, both now; Lord, have mercy twice; Lord, bless, with prostrations to the ground; leave out Remit, pardon and Forgive them that hate, and say only Lord, have mercy three times. Then read until the appointed hour, and afterward eat a little bread with kvass.
