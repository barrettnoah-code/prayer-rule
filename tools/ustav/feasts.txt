#   === short-name | Title        a dismissal (a Prayer on the site)
#   @ menaion M-D                 on this church (Julian) date; several allowed
#   @ sunday M-D..M-D             on the Sunday that falls between these dates
#   @ pascha N  /  N..M           this many days from Pascha (negative: before)
#     add service names to limit it: @ pascha 50 vespers
#   @ services all                every service (default: vespers matins hours)
#   @ on-sunday feast             also on a Sunday (default: the Sunday dismissal wins)
#   @ on-sunday other-short-name  on a Sunday, use that dismissal instead
#   @ lordly                      a great feast of the Lord (on a Sunday: prayer to the feast, not the Resurrection)
#   @ additions weekly            the daily additions stay as on an ordinary day (the Sundays of the fathers …)
#   @ fast lord|god-bearer|forerunner|icon|saint
#                                 what kind of feast it is for the Wednesday and Friday fast (fasting.txt
#                                 "@ feast" lines): the Lord's and the God-bearer's feasts release it for fish;
#                                 the Forerunner's other feasts for oil and wine. Icons and relics, and
#                                 the other feasts, also for oil and wine, without naming a category the
#                                 Ustav does not name
#   ! text                        rubric;  [saint] = the saint of the day
# The book's "…" endings are written out in full.

=== dismissal-sep-01 | Dismissal: Sep 1, the Indiction
@ fast saint
@ menaion 9-1
O Thou who by thine almighty and ineffable wisdom didst establish the beginning of the indiction, that is, of the new year: O Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, for the prayers of thy most pure Mother, and of her honorable and glorious synaxis in Miasena; and of our venerable father Symeon the Stylite and archimandrite, and of his mother Martha; and of the holy martyr Aithalas; and of the holy forty women, and of holy Ammon the deacon, their teacher; and of the holy martyrs Callistus, Evodus and Hermogenes, brothers according to the flesh; and of Joshua the son of Nun, and of all the saints, have mercy on us and save us, for thou art good and the lover of mankind.
